does not create rights that do not otherwise exist.

## Allowed descriptive use

You may accurately state that software is based on, compatible with, or forked from Vibe Racing and
link to the canonical public repository. You may use unmodified project names and marks in factual
commentary, criticism, education, or screenshots that do not imply sponsorship.

## Forks and distributions

Modified public services, connector binaries, packages, and installers must use a name and visual
identity that clearly distinguish them from official project releases. They must not imply that the
Vibe Racing maintainers reviewed, signed, operate, or support them. Attribution required by the
software and asset licenses must remain intact.

Official release signatures, domains, repository badges, and security claims may not be copied or
altered to make a third-party artifact appear official. Compatibility claims must identify the
tested version and must not claim OpenAI endorsement or verification.

## Visual assets

No official logo or distributable pixel-art asset set exists yet. Each future asset must have
documented authorship, source, license, allowed uses, and generated-asset disclosure where
applicable. Source-code licensing does not automatically license separately identified artwork.
