# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and
contributions of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste,
color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or
expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin,
socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of participation
are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## Encouraged behaviors

We agree to behave mindfully toward each other and act in ways that center our shared values,
including:

1. Respecting the purpose of our community and its ways of working.
2. Engaging kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Taking responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
6. Committing to repairing harm when it occurs.
7. Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the well-being of our community.

## Restricted behaviors

Instances, threats, and promotion of the following behaviors violate this Code of Conduct:

1. Harassment or violating explicitly expressed boundaries.
2. Insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a person or group.
3. Stereotyping or discrimination based on identity or traits.
4. Inappropriately sexualized behavior, language, or imagery.
5. Sharing or acting on another person's private information without permission.
6. Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
7. Impersonation, evading enforcement, or deliberately misleading identity.
8. Other conduct that threatens the safety or well-being of the community.

## Reporting an issue

Report possible violations privately to the project maintainer at `surkov.andrew@gmail.com`. Do not
open a public issue or include uninvolved people. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and as
confidentially as possible. Security vulnerabilities must instead use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit content, limit participation, issue a private warning, temporarily
suspend participation, or permanently ban a participant. The response will be proportionate to the
severity, impact, and pattern of the violation, with safety and repair prioritized over punishment.

The maintainer will avoid conflicts of interest. If a report concerns the maintainer and no neutral
project moderator is available, the reporter should use GitHub's abuse-reporting process.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the
project in public or other spaces.
