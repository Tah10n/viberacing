# Code of conduct

External participation status: closed.

Conduct reporting channel: not configured.

The repository is in pre-public preparation for an open-source community, but outside participation
must not be invited or accepted until a private conduct-reporting channel and at least one
accountable public maintainer are configured. This status is a publication safeguard, not an
invitation to report conduct concerns in public.

## Our commitment

Vibe Racing contributors, maintainers, and project representatives commit to a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free environment. Participation is evaluated on behavior and technical
contribution, without discrimination based on identity, background, experience, ability, or other
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly, patiently, and in good faith.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people.
- Respect consent, privacy, attribution, and the project's public-data boundary.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a project decision.
- Accept correction, repair harm where possible, and stop unwanted behavior promptly.
- Prioritize community and user safety over winning an argument or shipping quickly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing or soliciting another person's private information.
- Impersonation, deliberate deception, or manipulation of project processes.
- Coordinated disruption, spam, abusive automation, or retaliation against a reporter.
- Encouraging security disclosure through public issues or exposing confidential reports.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered unsafe or inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review systems, community spaces,
events, and private project communications. It also applies when someone represents Vibe Racing or
uses an official project role.

## Reporting and privacy

Do not report conduct incidents through a public issue, pull request, discussion, or social post. Do
not include credentials, private messages, unrelated personal data, or more evidence than is needed
to assess the incident.

While the conduct channel status above is `not configured`, the project is not ready to accept
outside participation. Before opening participation, maintainers must publish a project-controlled
private HTTPS reporting channel, document who can access it, test receipt and retention, and replace
both status lines at the top of this file. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct process.

## Enforcement

Authorized maintainers will review reports confidentially, disclose conflicts, preserve only the
minimum necessary records, and apply a proportionate response. Depending on severity and pattern,
responses may include a private correction, formal warning, temporary participation restriction,
permanent removal, or escalation to the hosting platform or appropriate authorities when safety or
law requires it.

No person should investigate a report about themselves. Retaliation against reporters, witnesses, or
responders is a separate violation. Public explanations should protect reporters and affected people
while giving the community enough information to understand material governance actions.
